## Our Mission

To create globally-viable frameworks for organizational and systemic change that enable **sustainable humanity transformation** through:

- **Absolute Truth** (Al-Haqq): Decision-making grounded in transparency and integrity
- **Bottom-Up Authority** (Inverted Pyramid): Power distributed to those closest to problems
- **Ethical AI Collaboration** (ICAM): Human judgment and AI insight working together

---

## Founding Principles

This work is guided by five commitments:

1. **Intellectual Honesty**: We acknowledge what we don't know and welcome challenge
2. **Evidence-Based Development**: Principles are grounded in philosophy AND practice
3. **Transparency**: All reasoning is visible; we explain our thinking
4. **Humility**: We assume we're wrong about some things; correction is welcome
5. **Accountability**: We stand behind what we say and do

## Decision-Making Process

### How We Make Decisions

**For documentation improvements**:
- Community suggests change
- Discussion in GitHub Issues/Discussions
- Core team evaluates
- If no objections → merged
- If significant objections → Advisory Council consultation

**For major framework changes**:
- Proposed in Discussion
- Community engagement (2 weeks minimum)
- Advisory Council evaluation
- Decision documented with reasoning
- Implementation with public timeline

**For conflict resolution**:
- Private discussion with all parties
- Seek common ground and misunderstanding first
- If unresolved → Advisory Council mediation
- Decision is made; implementation proceeds
- Minority perspective is documented (if wanted)

**For resource allocation**:
- Community prioritizes what matters most
- Founding team allocates accordingly
- Transparency about constraints

## Conflict of Interest & Transparency

### Our Commitments

- **Financial transparency**: Any funding sources are disclosed
- **Affiliation transparency**: Advisors' other roles are disclosed
- **Bias acknowledgment**: We name our biases; we don't hide them
- **Conflict resolution**: Conflicts are addressed openly, not behind the scenes

### Conflict Management

If a conflict emerges:

1. **Acknowledge it publicly** (or in private if sensitive)
2. **Understand all perspectives** before deciding
3. **Decide based on framework principles**: What does Al-Haqq, Inverted Pyramid, and ICAM suggest?
4. **Document the decision** with reasoning
5. **Move forward** (or agree to disagree and document both perspectives)

## Succession & Continuity

### Leadership Transition

Syams Maulana is the current lead. If unable to continue:

1. **Advisory Council decides** on interim leadership
2. **Interim leader** runs organization for 6 months
3. **Community input** on permanent leader
4. **Collective decision** on next leader
5. **Transition period**: 3 months overlap

### Knowledge Preservation

- All decisions are documented
- Reasoning is preserved (not just outcomes)
- Institutional knowledge lives in documentation, not people
- Multiple people understand each domain

### Long-term Vision

This is designed to outlive any individual leader. The framework should evolve into a movement where:

- The founding team is unnecessary (but still valued)
- Leadership is distributed globally
- The framework is maintained by the community
- Power is truly inverted
